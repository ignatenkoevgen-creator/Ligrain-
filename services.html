Our Services

Integrated logistics from origin to destination.

Our services are designed to work together. Depending on the shipment, Ligrain can coordinate one individual element or manage the complete logistics chain from origin through transshipment and onward delivery.

Integrated model

Rail. Road. Terminals. Warehouses.

Each element is coordinated as part of one operational solution rather than treated as an isolated service.

Core services

Six logistics capabilities. One coordinated chain.

The exact combination depends on the cargo, route and operational conditions.

01

Rail Freight

Coordination of international railway transportation across European corridors, including block-train operations and cross-border movements.

  • Rail route planning and operator coordination
  • Block-train and wagon movement management
  • Border and timetable coordination
  • Connection with terminals and receiving points
02

Road Freight

Road transportation for direct movements as well as first-mile and last-mile connections supporting rail and multimodal logistics.

  • International road delivery
  • First-mile and last-mile transport
  • Terminal-to-warehouse delivery
  • Flexible support for multimodal routes
03

Cargo Transshipment

Coordination of cargo transfer between railway gauges, wagons and transport modes at strategic border and terminal locations.

  • Broad-gauge / standard-gauge interface coordination
  • Terminal handling arrangements
  • Wagon-to-wagon or modal transfer
  • Operational sequencing and timing
04

Multimodal Logistics

Combined transport chains integrating rail, road, terminal and port infrastructure where a single transport mode is not sufficient.

  • Rail + road combinations
  • Terminal and port integration
  • Alternative route development
  • End-to-end operational coordination
05

Terminal & Warehouse Solutions

Coordination of cargo handling, inland storage and delivery through partner terminals and warehouse facilities close to European destinations.

  • Terminal receiving and handling
  • Temporary or operational storage
  • Warehouse coordination
  • Final-mile dispatch to receiver
06

Operational Coordination

The central service behind all Ligrain logistics solutions: one team coordinating the parties, schedule, information flow and execution.

  • Single operational point of contact
  • Carrier and terminal communication
  • Schedule and movement coordination
  • Operational issue management
