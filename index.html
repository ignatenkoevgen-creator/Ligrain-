About Ligrain

Your logistics partner across Europe.

Ligrain provides integrated logistics solutions for international cargo movements across Central, Eastern and Southern Europe. We coordinate shippers, railway operators, road carriers, terminals, transshipment points, warehouses and receivers — giving the client one operational point of contact for the entire movement.

01 Rail Freight

International rail movements, block-train coordination and cross-border operations.

02 Road Freight

Flexible road solutions for first-mile, last-mile and direct international delivery.

03 Transshipment

Gauge-change, terminal handling and cargo transfer coordination at key border points.

04 Warehousing

Inland terminal and warehouse coordination close to final European destinations.
